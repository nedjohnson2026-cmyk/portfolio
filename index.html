About me

Builder, tinkerer, engineer-in-training

I'm an Electrical Engineering student at Rutgers University–New Brunswick (Class of 2029). I like building things that move, light up, and respond, and I enjoy owning the whole project: CAD and 3D printing, wiring and soldering, and the embedded code that ties it together.

It started in high school with FIRST Robotics. As Vice President of Technology and Design, I founded our team's CAD/CNC sub-team, directed robot design and fabrication across sub-teams, and helped lead a 60-person team to its first-ever FIRST Robotics International Competition.

Since then I've built a voice-controlled robotic arm with a Raspberry Pi and Arduino, an RC car that navigates randomly, and a fitness app I'm preparing to publish on the App Store. I also like sharing what I've learned: I mentored a FIRST LEGO League team and taught kids programming, robotics, and game design at Black Rocket.
